    How offline works

    When the network drops, the counter keeps going.

    Joryka is designed offline-first, so the everyday work of your shop doesn’t have to wait for a signal.

    • Keep serving customersRecord sales at the counter, even when connectivity is unreliable.

    • Keep your records togetherSales, stock and customer credit stay in one system — not scattered across notebooks.

    • Always know your statusA clear connection indicator shows whether you are online, and how many changes are waiting to sync.
